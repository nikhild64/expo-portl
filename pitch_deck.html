01 • THE PROBLEM

Residential Communities Suffer From Friction

Traditional society management leads to security gaps, delayed payments, and unorganized communication.

🚪

Gate Bottlenecks

Paper registers and endless phone calls cause long queues at entry gates and missed visitor approvals.

💳

Billing Chaos

Treasurers spend hours tracking cash, cheques, and manual receipts for monthly maintenance dues.

🎾

Amenity Conflicts

Shared facilities like clubhouses or courts are booked verbally, leading to double bookings and confusion.

💬

Scattered Alerts

Important announcements get buried in noisy WhatsApp groups, while complaints go untracked.

02 • THE SOLUTION (1/3)

Resident App: Empowering Every Homeowner

1-Tap Gate Approvals, Pre-Approved Guest Passes & Integrated Digital Payments.

  • Instant 1-Tap Approvals: Instant push notifications when guests or deliveries arrive at the gate.
  • Pre-Approved QR Passes: Share WhatsApp QR passes for fast-track guest entry.
  • Razorpay Dues Integration: Clear maintenance bills securely with instant digital receipting.
  • Slot Reservations: Reserve clubhouse & sports facilities with live calendar availability.
02 • THE SOLUTION (2/3)

Security Guard App: Lightweight Gate Control

Fast Visitor Registration, Camera QR Scanning & Real-Time Gate Sync.

  • Instant Visitor Logging: Quick entry form for walk-in visitors and delivery agents.
  • QR Camera Scanner: Scans guest pre-approval passes with atomic database verification.
  • Real-time Gate Feed: Immediate visual confirmation when a resident approves entry.
  • Zero Paper Registers: Eliminates physical logbooks for a 100% digital security gate.
02 • THE SOLUTION (3/3)

Admin Dashboard: Complete Society Command

Automated Dues Generation, Notices, Interactive Polls & Grievance Tracking.

  • Automated Monthly Billing: Generate dues cycles across all flats in a single click.
  • Notices & Interactive Polls: Broadcast community updates and conduct resident voting.
  • Grievance Resolution: Track, assign, and resolve resident complaint tickets transparently.
  • Live Analytics: Real-time charts on visitor traffic, active maintenance, and payment rates.
